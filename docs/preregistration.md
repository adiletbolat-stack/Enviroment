**RQ2.** H0: marker frequency per 1,000 words does not differ between users with < 1 and > 3 months of use.
H1 (directional): users with > 3 months show higher frequency. Test: one-sided Mann-Whitney U at alpha = 0.05; effect size Cliff's delta with bootstrap 95% CI. Secondary: trend across < 1, 1-3, > 3 months (Jonckheere-Terpstra).

**RQ3.** H0: rho = 0. H1: positive Spearman correlation between duration/intensity and the isolation-avoidance index.

**Unit of analysis:** author (markers and words pooled across posts, then normalised per 1,000 words).
**Inclusion:** English posts with an explicit statement of duration of use; at least 100 words per post.
**Reliability:** second coder on a random 10-20% subsample; Cohen's kappa >= 0.6 required, otherwise the codebook is revised.
