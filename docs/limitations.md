# Limitations

- Cross-sectional, observational design: associations only, no causal claims (reverse causality: lonely users may stay with an AI companion longer).
- Self-selection of emotionally engaged posters; duration of use and student status are self-reported.
- Single platform family and language (English); survivorship: dissatisfied users stop posting.
- Excluded posts (missing values, below the minimum length, deleted/removed, group conflicts) are reported by reason in every analysis run.
